Tips

What makes a loop worth watching

01
Keep it under three seconds

Short windows read as a loop. Anything longer starts to feel like a clip that stutters.

02
Cut around the peak

Put the jump, the pour or the turn in the middle of your window so the reverse lands on it too.

03
Hold the camera still

A steady frame makes the reverse invisible. Panning shots fight the loop.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What is a boomerang, exactly?

A short clip that plays forward, then backward, then repeats. It has no start or end, so it reads as a loop in a feed.

Do you store my videos?

Trimming and preview happen in your browser, so the free path never uploads anything. Paid exports are rendered on a server and deleted within an hour.

Which formats can I upload?

MP4, MOV and WebM, up to 200 MB. Exports come out as MP4; GIF is available on the unlimited plan.

How does the subscription work?

€4.99 a month for unlimited 1080p and GIF exports. It renews monthly until you cancel, and there is no minimum term.

How do I cancel?

One button in your account page, or reply to your receipt email. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.
